Rendering rules

  • GRID LAW. Every mark snaps to one square cell — no anti-aliasing, no gradients. Mid-tones exist only as 50/50 dither checkers of two palette colors.
  • LIGHT. One source, high and left: a single solid highlight step, a single dithered shade step, then void. Nothing softer ships.
  • LINE. No outer black stroke — silhouettes cut hard against Void Navy, and interior color fields separate with 1-cell navy seams (canopy frames, engine gaps).
  • SHAPE. Chunky, symmetric, 45°/90° stair-steps only. Green/white is player tech, magenta is threat, orange-to-white is energy — readable at one glance from three feet.
