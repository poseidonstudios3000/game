Rendering rules

  1. Stroke, never fill. Every ship, alien, and HUD element is a closed luminous outline on the void; interiors stay empty so the field reads through everything — the one sanctioned fill is a filament-white hot core (rule 04).
  2. Light is the material. Forms are self-luminous — no external light, no cast shadows. Every stroke carries two glow passes: a wide soft halo under a tight hot line, like an overdriven vector beam.
  3. Hard geometric shape language. The player is a single forward wedge; enemies are down-pointing fangs and cellular hexes. Straight edges and pure primitives only — nothing organic.
  4. Color means faction. Ion cyan is always the player, magenta and violet are always hostile, and filament white is reserved for the hottest points on screen: cores, hits, and pickups.
