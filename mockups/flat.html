SPRITE STUDIES

RENDER RULES

  • One line. Every sprite and HUD pill wears the same thick twilight-ink outline; sky layers go lineless so they fall back.
  • Two tones, hard edge. Flat fill plus exactly one darker shade per shape, cut on a straight or crescent edge. Light lives top-left. No gradients, ever.
  • Shape language. Pills, blobs and circles only — if a corner is not rounded, it does not ship. Enemies get pupils, antennae and tiny frowns.
  • Depth is shadow. Layers separate with soft oversized indigo drop shadows, never with line weight, texture or grit.
