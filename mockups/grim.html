Rendering rules

  • 01NO OUTLINES. Forms read by value against the void; panel seams are gaps of Void Black, never strokes of light.
  • 02ONE KEY LIGHT. Everything is lit from screen-top: hulls grade Bone Steel into Gun Slate, alien flesh falls off into Oxblood.
  • 03SHAPE LANGUAGE. The gunship is chamfered and symmetric; everything alien is ribbed, curved, and deliberately asymmetric. Threat must read in silhouette at 16 px.
  • 04PLASMA IS EMISSION. #FF6A2B appears only where energy burns — engines, hostile munitions, alien eyes. Your guns run cold: tracers grade Bone Steel into white. If it is orange and inbound, it kills you. All other color is a tint or shade of the five swatches.
